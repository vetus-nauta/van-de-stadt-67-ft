## Research policy

1. Designer drawings/data beat marketplace listings.
2. Physical measurement of the current hull beats all marketplace dimensions.
3. Related Van de Stadt yachts are references only; their equipment is never to be presented as No. 430 original equipment.
4. Any future recommended component must be backed by a calculation or explicit operational requirement.
5. Conflicting values stay visible until resolved; do not silently average them.
