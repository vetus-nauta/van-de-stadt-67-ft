## 3. Critical mass conflict

The original No. 430 design has a published **51 t displacement and 19 t ballast**. The unfinished Gdansk hull listing states **11 t dry weight and 15 t ballast**.

These figures cannot be treated as equivalent states of the vessel. Most likely the 11 t figure describes the unfinished aluminium shell/structure or an incomplete lightship state, not a fully completed yacht. This must be resolved before naval architecture calculations.

### Mandatory actions

- Obtain a calibrated travel-lift/crane weight if the hull can be weighed.
- Record all components present during weighing.
- Identify ballast material: lead, iron, mixed or structural keel.
- Determine whether the advertised 15 t ballast is installed, planned, or nominal.
- Establish longitudinal location of ballast CG.
- Build an itemised lightship weight estimate before interior fit-out.

## 4. Weight-control structure for future project

Maintain a live weight ledger with at least:

| Group | Typical contents |
|---|---|
| Structure | shell, deck, bulkheads, frames, stringers, keel structure, rudder |
| Ballast | fixed ballast, trim ballast |
| Rig | mast, boom, standing rigging, running rigging, sails |
| Propulsion | engine, gearbox, shaft, propeller, exhaust, fuel day system |
| Electrical | batteries, inverter/chargers, generator, cabling, transformers |
| Plumbing | tanks, pumps, watermaker, boilers, piping |
| HVAC | chillers/air handlers or packaged units, heating |
| Deck equipment | windlass, anchors, chain, winches, davits, passerelle |
| Interior | joinery, flooring, insulation, glazing, furniture |
| Navigation/comms | displays, autopilot, radar, radios, antennas |
| Safety | fire system, liferafts, bilge system, emergency gear |
| Consumables | fuel, fresh water, stores, spares |
| Crew/guests | design payload |

For each item store mass, longitudinal coordinate, transverse coordinate, vertical coordinate, source and confidence.

## 5. Structural survey points

### Aluminium shell
- Plate alloy and temper.
- Bottom, topside, deck and superstructure plate thickness by zone.
- Frame spacing and scantlings.
- Longitudinal stiffener spacing and profile.
- Weld type and accessible weld quality.
- Distortion/fairness.
- Dissimilar-metal interfaces.
- Corrosion/pitting, especially bilge and future machinery area.

### High-load nodes
- Mast step and compression post/structure.
- Chainplate attachments and load paths.
- Keel attachment / keel box structure.
- Rudder stock bearings and foundations.
- Engine beds.
- Shaft tube / stern tube provisions.
- Windlass foundation.
- Forestay and backstay structure.
- Davit/tender garage/transom structure if applicable.

### Watertight integrity
- Collision bulkhead.
- Machinery-space boundaries.
- Aft lazarette/garage boundary.
- Through-hull locations.
- Sea chests, if any.
- Existing penetrations below waterline.

## 6. Stability information required

No public hydrostatic booklet, GZ curve or inclining-test data for No. 430 has been located yet. Required before major design decisions:

- Lines plan / 3D hull surface.
- Hydrostatics at several displacements.
- LCB, VCB, LCF.
- KMt/KMl.
- Righting-arm curves for relevant loading conditions.
- Downflooding angle.
- Sail-carrying assumptions.
- Ballast CG.
- Tank free-surface moments.

Until those are recovered or recreated, equipment placement should be treated as provisional.
