## Rig architecture — project decisions to recover/freeze

- Sloop vs cutter configuration.
- Mast material: aluminium vs carbon.
- Keel-stepped vs deck-stepped mast.
- Fractional vs masthead forestay geometry.
- Inner forestay/staysail provision.
- Backstay arrangement.
- Standing rigging type: wire, rod, compact strand or synthetic.
- Mainsail handling: slab reefing, in-boom, in-mast.
- Headsail furling system and emergency manual operation.
- Storm sail attachment points.

## Closely related reference: Stadtship 70

The 21.0 m aluminium Van de Stadt Stadtship 70 uses:

- Carbon mast.
- Carbon Park Avenue boom.
- Sloop rig.
- Published sail area 230 m².
- 90 m² genoa (published later-sale inventory).
- Approx. 40 m² cutter sail.
- Gennaker with snuffer.
- Manual furling for genoa/cutter, operated using powered winches.
- 2 Harken winches at mast and 4 electric Harken primary winches in working cockpit.
- Halyards led aft below deck to the working cockpit.

**REFERENCE only.** It shows a proven systems philosophy for a yacht of essentially the same length, but is not original No. 430 equipment.

## Deck arrangement concepts worth preserving

A useful Van de Stadt large-cruiser pattern is separation between:

1. **Working cockpit** — helm, sheets, halyards, primary winches and sail controls.
2. **Protected guest cockpit / deck-saloon interface** — seating protected by doghouse/deck saloon.

For the future 67 ft layout, this separation should be evaluated against the actual deck geometry before locating winches or cutting deck penetrations.

## Ground tackle — sizing work package

No confirmed No. 430 anchor/windlass specification found yet. The final system should be sized from actual displacement and intended operating area, not from LOA alone.

Required design inputs:

- Full-load displacement.
- Maximum expected anchoring depth.
- Mediterranean vs ocean cruising profile.
- Primary anchor type and target mass.
- Chain diameter, grade and length.
- Secondary anchor arrangement.
- Windlass working load and maximum pull.
- Hawse pipe geometry and chain fall.
- Chain locker volume, drainage and access.
- Windlass foundation reinforcement.

## Survey measurements required before rig order

- Mast step coordinates and foundation dimensions.
- Chainplate coordinates in 3 axes.
- Stem/forestay fitting geometry.
- Backstay attachment geometry.
- Boom clearance to deck saloon/doghouse.
- Actual sheer/deck camber.
- Winch foundation positions.
- All mast penetration and conduit routes.
