# 08 — Open Questions, Measurements & Document Recovery

This is the practical acquisition/survey checklist. The purpose is to turn marketplace-level information into a usable engineering basis.

## Priority A — original Van de Stadt documentation

Request specifically for **Design No. 430 / Van de Stadt 67**:

- General arrangement.
- Lines plan / offsets / hull geometry.
- Construction plan and aluminium scantlings.
- Frame and longitudinal schedules.
- Bulkhead plan.
- Keel construction and ballast drawing.
- Rudder and stock drawing.
- Mast step and chainplate drawings.
- Sail plan with I/J/P/E and stay geometry.
- Hydrostatic calculations.
- Stability / righting moment information.
- Engine/shaft-line arrangement if originally specified.
- Tank plan.
- System schematics if they were part of the package.
- Design displacement definition and loading assumptions.

## Priority B — physical hull dimensional survey

### External geometry
- LOA actual.
- LWL reference marks if present.
- Beam max and location.
- Draft hull-only and with ballast condition.
- Freeboard at bow/midships/stern.
- Keel chord, span, thickness profile and bulb/ballast geometry.
- Rudder dimensions and balance area.
- Propeller aperture.

### Internal structure
- Frame station spacing.
- Frame profiles and dimensions.
- Longitudinal profile and spacing.
- Plate thickness map: bottom / turn of bilge / topsides / deck / superstructure.
- Main bulkhead positions from a single longitudinal datum.
- Watertight bulkhead status.
- Mast foundation geometry.
- Chainplate foundations.
- Keel floors / keel attachment structure.
- Engine beds.
- Stern tube / shaft-line provisions.
- Rudder bearing foundations.

## Priority C — weight

- Calibrated current total weight.
- Exact condition during weighing.
- Ballast installed? yes/no.
- Ballast material.
- Ballast mass by documentation or weighing.
- Major loose items included in 11 t listing figure.

Create a weight ledger before purchasing heavy equipment.

## Priority D — tanks

For each existing tank:

- Function.
- Port/starboard/centre position.
- Fore/aft limits.
- Height/width/length or irregular geometry.
- Calculated volume.
- Inspection hatch.
- Baffles.
- Fill/vent/pickup/return fittings.
- Whether integral with hull structure or separate.

Resolve current listing claim: 2 fuel + 2 fresh-water + 1 black-water tank.

## Priority E — machinery geometry

Measure engine room in 3D:

- Maximum length/width/height for main engine.
- Service clearance to filters, impeller, belts, injectors and gearbox.
- Engine removal route.
- Generator footprint(s).
- Exhaust path and waterlock position.
- Seawater intake/sea-chest position.
- Fuel filter/manifold space.
- Battery space and ventilation/fire separation.
- Watermaker/chiller/hydraulic pack locations.

## Priority F — shaft and propeller

- Existing shaft tube ID/OD and angle.
- Distance gearbox flange to stern bearing.
- Propeller centreline to hull.
- Maximum physically possible prop diameter with safe tip clearance.
- Rudder-to-prop clearance.
- Space for shaft seal and thrust bearing if needed.

## Priority G — rig interfaces

- Mast step station and transverse position.
- Mast step dimensions.
- Chainplate locations in X/Y/Z.
- Forestay fitting coordinate.
- Backstay attachment coordinates.
- Deck saloon roof clearance to boom.
- Any existing deck reinforcement for winches/tracks.

## Priority H — deck openings and glazing

- Every hatch opening.
- Companionway dimensions.
- Deck-saloon glazing openings and thickness assumptions.
- Emergency escape openings.
- Anchor locker and chain fall geometry.
- Tender/garage/transom opening if present.

## Questions to seller/builder

1. Is the hull genuinely built from Van de Stadt No. 430 drawings, and what revision?
2. Why does beam differ between official No. 430 list (5.50 m) and current listings (5.75–5.80 m)?
3. Why does draft differ between official design 2.50 m and current listings 2.8–3.0 m?
4. What exactly does the published 11,000 kg dry weight include?
5. Is the published 15,000 kg ballast installed or planned?
6. What ballast material is used?
7. Are the tanks already fabricated? If yes, what are capacities and test records?
8. What aluminium alloy/temper and plate thicknesses were used?
9. Are welding procedure qualifications and material certificates available?
10. Are NDT reports available for critical welds?
11. Is there a build book/photo record by station?
12. Are engine beds and shaft line already fixed?
13. Is rudder/stock included?
14. Are chainplates and mast step included and built to original drawings?
15. What exactly is covered by the advertised 2-year hull warranty?

## Deliverables after survey

- dimensioned GA,
- structure station map,
- tank plan,
- machinery-space envelope drawing,
- weight ledger,
- photo index by station,
- list of deviations from No. 430 drawings,
- red/yellow/green structural punch list,
- first-pass systems architecture.
