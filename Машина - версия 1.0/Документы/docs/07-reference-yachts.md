# 07 — Reference Van de Stadt Yachts

This file separates genuine No. 430 evidence from nearby Van de Stadt designs that are useful only as engineering references.

## A. Van de Stadt 67 — Design No. 430

Primary target design.

- Year: 1992.
- LOA: 20.50 m.
- LWL: 17.50 m.
- Beam: 5.50 m in current official design list; marketplace data for current hull gives 5.75–5.80 m.
- Draft: 2.50 m official design value.
- Displacement: 51 t.
- Ballast: 19 t.
- Sail area: 241 m².
- Material: steel / aluminium.
- Concept: comfortable cruising yacht with deck saloon.

No builder is listed in the Van de Stadt design archive for No. 430.

- LWL 17.50 m.
- Beam 5.75 m on boats.com; 5.80 m on BoatNet.
- Draft 3.0 m on boats.com; 2.8 m on BoatNet.
- Freeboard ~1.8 m.
- Dry/current weight 11,000 kg listing value.
- Ballast 15,000 kg listing value.
- Total sail area 241 m².
- Main 131 m².
- Headsail 110 m².
- Air draft / bridge clearance ~32 m.
- 3 guest cabins.
- 3 guest heads.
- 2 fuel tanks, 2 water tanks, 1 black-water tank; capacities not published.

The hull listing values contain conflicts with original No. 430 design data, so physical survey and original drawings take precedence.

## D. Stadtship 70 — Design No. 670

Extremely useful published systems reference because it is a complete 21 m aluminium Van de Stadt cruising yacht.

### Principal dimensions
- LOA 21.00 m.
- LWL 18.60 m.
- Beam 5.10 m.
- Draft 2.90 m.
- Displacement 29 t.
- Sail area 230 m².
- Aluminium.
- Lead-ballasted bulb keel.
- Water ballast 2 × 1500 L.

### Propulsion
- Perkins Sabre M150Ti.
- 148 hp / 108.9 kW.
- ZF45A hydraulic gearbox.
- Stainless shaft Ø50 mm.
- 4-blade folding propeller.
- Approx. 10 kn max / 8.5 kn cruise.
- Retractable bow thruster, pneumatic powered.

### Electrical
- 12/24/220 V.
- 24 V 1000 Ah service bank.
- 120 A Mastervolt alternator.
- Onan 7.5 kVA generator.
- Mastervolt 24/3500-100 inverter/charger.
- 3 isolation transformers.
- Fixed Watt & Sea hydrogenerator.

### Tanks / water
- Fuel approx. 1150 L total incl. day tank.
- Water 2 × 1500 L wing tanks + 500 L day tank.
- Waste tank 300 L.
- Spectra Newport 400 watermaker, ~64 L/h.
- 2 hot-water boilers.

### Rig/deck
- Carbon mast.
- Carbon Park Avenue boom.
- 90 m² genoa.
- ~40 m² cutter sail.
- Gennaker.
- 2 Harken mast winches + 4 electric Harken cockpit winches.
- Halyards led aft below deck.
- Two-cockpit concept.

### Accommodation
- 4 staterooms / 8 people.
- 3 bathrooms.
- Separate engineering area.
- 2.75 m tender stored through aft/transom hatch.

## F. Van de Stadt 84 — Design No. 646 / later complete yacht reference

Useful only for heavy-yacht systems scaling and arrangement ideas.

Published later-sale specification includes:

- 3 aluminium fuel tanks, total 2350 L.
- 3 × 500 L fresh-water tanks.
- 3 × 90 L black-water tanks.
- Grey-water tanks total approx. 1000 L.
- Lewmar Mamba mechanical steering, 2 outside helms.
- Spray-foam insulation.
- Diesel central heating and master-cabin air conditioning.

## Transferability rule

A reference-yacht component can enter the future Van de Stadt 67 specification only after checking:

1. displacement and resistance,
2. actual available space,
3. electrical/hydraulic architecture,
4. weight and CG impact,
5. class/flag/ISO requirements,
6. present-day service/support availability.
