# Draft RFQ — VDS67 propulsion package and generator interfaces

Revision M06/v001 · 11 September 2026 · Prepared for review; not sent.

Project: completion of an existing unpainted aluminium Van de Stadt 67 hull in Poland. This is a request for an itemised technical and commercial proposal, not an order. Yard address, flag, intended use and buyer VAT details will follow. Where an engineering input is pending, identify it and provide a conditional proposal with explicit assumptions.

## Scope and quantities

One main propulsion line: **one engine and one matched marine gearbox**, including the items required for installation and commissioning. Please price the following as mutually exclusive alternatives, keeping quantities unchanged:

- Yanmar 6LPA-STP2 / dealer-described 6LPAM-STP2 bobtail with a matched KMH50A-LP. Confirm the exact engine identity, production year, condition, serial/configuration code and applicability to this completion project in the EU.
- Yanmar 8LV320 inboard package with a matched KMH50A and VC20. Confirm the supplied control-system revision and complete bill of materials; older references mention VC10.

Approximately 300 metric hp is an inherited search target, not an approved power requirement. Required speed, resistance, displacement in service, annual hours and load profile remain to be established. State rated and continuous/cruise output, permitted duty cycle, derating and restrictions. Do not treat equal advertised horsepower as equal continuous performance. State the additional power available for hydraulic consumers while propulsion is loaded and at manoeuvring speed.

Two marine generators are retained in the project, currently considering Fischer Panda 12000x or 15000i as alternative pairs. If these are outside your supply scope, mark them as excluded and supply the propulsion interface requirements. Do not quote four generators or assume parallel operation.

## Required technical response

1. Complete the item-by-item supplier response against the IDs in the attached M06 schedule. Mark each row **included / separately priced / excluded / optional / clarification required**. For included items, identify the parent package and quantity. Provide exact part numbers, supply boundaries and all omissions. Do not add an item already included in another package.
2. Provide the gearbox ratio options and rating, output flange, flywheel/housing interface, damper, mounting feet, flexible mounts, oil cooler, hoses, fittings and shift hardware. Select a final ratio only after the propeller and shaft calculation. Identify requirements for shaft alignment, thrust transfer and torsional compatibility.
3. Provide complete engine controls, panel, extension cables and connectors, throttle/shift controls, alarm/stop functions and start-in-gear interlock. Confirm the exact 6LPA harness revision; 129574-77720 and 129574-77760 are not assumed interchangeable. Cable lengths will be confirmed from routing.
4. The owner's requirement is **24 V DC and 220 V AC**. Please confirm a factory or manufacturer-approved 24 V starting/control/charging solution for the exact offered engine and generators, with part numbers, current demand, wiring and warranty. Standard 12 V equipment is not accepted as evidence of compliance. State whether nominal 230 V / 50 Hz generator equipment can meet the required AC service and its permitted voltage range; 50 Hz is currently an inherited project target.
5. Retain exactly **six service batteries, two generator starting batteries and one main-engine starting battery**. The service battery description is currently “115 amperes each”; capacity unit, individual battery voltage, chemistry and cranking capability are unresolved. Provide the information needed to design remote backup starting from other healthy sources, isolating a failed battery before transfer. Do not assume unrestricted battery paralleling or increase battery count without an explicit alternative proposal. Demonstrate every supported source-to-starter path and identify unsupported paths.
6. Thrusters and all winches will be hydraulic. State approved PTO interfaces, allowable continuous/intermittent power and speed, torque and belt/shaft loads, pump/drive requirements and effects on engine warranty. Hydraulic load pressures, flows and simultaneous-use scenarios are pending. A hydraulic gearbox alone does not establish a PTO suitable for these consumers. Identify provisions required for low-speed and engine-unavailable operation.
7. Provide cooling-water flow, inlet restrictions, exhaust backpressure and temperature limits, waterline/heel installation limits, ventilation/heat rejection, fuel feed/return restrictions and required alarms/shutdowns. Include installation diagrams for the proposed cooling and exhaust arrangements. Generator external branches must preserve the intended independence of the two sets.
8. Supply a dimensioned installation GA and STEP model for the exact package, including external boxes, pipes and mounting points. Show minimum service clearances, access to impellers/filters/belts, removal paths, lifting points, dry and operating mass, centre of gravity and foundation loads. Room fit has not yet been verified; a catalogue bounding box is insufficient.
9. Provide the declarations/certificates applicable to the exact engines and intended EU installation, installation manuals, warranty terms, commissioning requirements and local Poland/EU service arrangements. Please identify the information required from the yard to establish applicability rather than assuming an older certificate covers this build.

## Price and delivery response

Quote unit and line prices **excluding VAT**, in a stated currency, and list any VAT separately with its basis. State quotation date/validity, dispatch country and location, condition/new-or-stock status, availability, lead time, warranty and Incoterms with named place. Identify whether goods are in EU free circulation. Quote freight, insurance, export/import handling where applicable, installation, alignment, commissioning and acceptance tests separately. Do not assume the buyer's VAT recovery entitlement or invent a delivered Poland total without a destination and scope.

Public website prices are reference evidence only. In particular, the Palmer Power KMH50A-LP reference contains an old validity date in its metadata; please replace it with a dated written quotation for the offered configuration. Return a complete inclusion/exclusion list even if some final amounts remain conditional.
